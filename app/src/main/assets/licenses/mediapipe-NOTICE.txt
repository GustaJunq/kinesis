Used for background removal, which runs entirely on the device. The model (assets/models/selfie_segmenter.tflite,
"selfie_segmenter", float16, 256x256) comes from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/image_segmenter/selfie_segmenter/float16/latest/selfie_segmenter.tflite
and its model card states the Apache 2.0 license. It separates people from the background; other subjects are not cut out.
https://ai.google.dev/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter

The Roto Brush uses the Interactive Segmenter with the Magic Touch model (assets/models/magic_touch.tflite, 512x512, float32), from
https://storage.googleapis.com/mediapipe-models/interactive_segmenter/magic_touch/float32/1/magic_touch.tflite
Its model card (MagicTouch, Valentin Bazarevsky and Ben Hahn, Google, 2023) states: "Licensed under Apache License, Version 2.0".
It finds the object under one point of the picture, whatever the object is.
